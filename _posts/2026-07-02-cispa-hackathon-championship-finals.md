After qualifying from the [Vienna regional round]({% post_url 2025-12-14-european-cybersecurity-ai-hackathon %}) in December, my teammates Tanja Šarčević, Yelyzaveta Klysa, Sabina Khazari, and I, team "4 braincells", traveled to CISPA for the grand final of the European Cybersecurity & AI Hackathon Championship, joining 16 other regional winners from Paris, Vienna, Stockholm, Munich, Barcelona, and Warsaw.

{% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/highlights/cispa-hackathon-finals-2026.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" alt="Our team at the European Cybersecurity & AI Hackathon Championship finals" caption="Celebrating a 4th-place finish with team 4 braincells." %}

We placed in the top 6 by score and advanced to the presentation round, which lifted us to 4th overall. The finals featured three challenges: localizing watermarked text regions, targeted attacks to misclassify training-set members, non-members, and generated images, and reconstructing face images from gradients.

Many thanks to Adam Dziedzic and Franziska Boenisch for organizing such a unique event, and to my teammates for a great run together. More details are available on the [official CISPA Hackathon Championship page](https://cispa.de/en/cispa-hackathon-championship).
