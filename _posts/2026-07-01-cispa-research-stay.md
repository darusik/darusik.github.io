At the turn of June and July, I visited the [Dormant Neurons](https://dormantneurons.science/) group at CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security for a short research exchange (June 30–July 1), hosted by [Lea Schönherr](https://leaschoenherr.me/). The group works on making AI systems more trustworthy, with a focus on generative and agentic systems.

{% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/highlights/cispa-research-stay-2026.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded" alt="With the Dormant Neurons group at CISPA" caption="With the Dormant Neurons group at CISPA." %}

Over two intense days we exchanged research interests and directions, and I gave a talk on rethinking long-standing assumptions in model stealing research. I am grateful to Lea and the group for the warm welcome and the many thoughtful discussions.
